# Bounded novelty note: additive 2-matroids of rank at most four

**Search date:** 2 November 2026. **Scope:** the supplementary analytic six-element inequality and its immediate rank and ground-set consequences. This note supplements, rather than replaces, the main `novelty_report.md`. It records a bounded public-literature search; it does not certify historical priority.

## 1. Frozen statement and conventions

An **additive 2-matroid** here is a nonempty downset \(\mathcal I\subseteq2^V\) satisfying

\[
S,T\in\mathcal I,\quad |T|\ge |S|+2
\quad\Longrightarrow\quad
S\cup\{x\}\in\mathcal I\text{ for some }x\in T\setminus S.
\]

This is the definition in Xie–Xu, Proposition 4.2 and the following paragraph [XX26], with augmentation parameter two. It is unrelated to the use of “2-matroid” for a Bouchet multimatroid whose skew classes have size two. It is also distinct from “rank-two matroid” and “2-polymatroid.” The rank \(r\) means \(\max_{S\in\mathcal I}|S|\), whereas \(n=|V|\) is the ground-set size.

For activities \(w_v\ge0\), put

\[
a_k(w)=\sum_{S\in\mathcal I,\ |S|=k}\prod_{v\in S}w_v.
\]

The supplement's claims are:

1. If \(r\le4\), the product-weight measure has CAPP, and \((a_k(w)/\binom nk)_{k=0}^n\) is log-concave with no internal zeros.
2. The same conclusions hold when \(n\le7\), with no rank restriction.
3. For arbitrary \(r,n\), the initial segment with indices \(0,\ldots,\min(4,n)\) is log-concave with the **ambient** binomial normalization \(\binom nk\). Thus the nontrivial asserted inequalities have centres \(k=1,2,3\), where defined.

These statements do not settle the unrestricted additive 2-matroid conjecture. In particular, the six-element lemma supplies no eight-element middle APP inequality.

## 2. Exact local formula and finite fingerprints

On a ground set of size \(2k\), define

\[
F_j=\{S\in\mathcal I:|S|=j,\ V\setminus S\in\mathcal I\}.
\]

The APP inequality is

\[
\frac{|F_k|}{\binom{2k}{k}}\ge
\frac{|F_{k-1}|}{\binom{2k}{k-1}},
\qquad k|F_k|\ge(k+1)|F_{k-1}|.
\]

At six elements it becomes \(3|F_3|\ge4|F_2|\). In unordered balanced-partition notation \(m=|F_3|/2\), \(r'=|F_2|\), this is \(3m\ge2r'\). The normalization and ordered/unordered factor of two matter for formula searches. Product activities multiply every nonzero antipodal product by the same factor; they do not create a separate local counting problem.

The following exact examples were generated directly from their feasible sets and checked for the gap-two axiom during this gate.

| System | Rank counts, including zeros | \((|F_2|,|F_3|)\) on six elements | Margin \(3|F_3|-4|F_2|\) |
|---|---|---|---|
| Downset generated by \(\{1,2\}\) and \(\{3\}\) | \((1,3,1,0)\) | Not applicable | Not applicable |
| Uniform matroid \(U_{4,6}\) | \((1,6,15,20,15,0,0)\) | \((15,20)\) | \(0\) |
| Noncover downset of three disjoint edges | \((1,6,15,12,3,0,0)\) | \((3,12)\) | \(24\) |
| Rank-two downset with feasible pairs the nonedges of \(C_5\) | \((1,5,5,0,0,0)\) | Not applicable | Not applicable |

The third example has generating polynomial \(1+6t+15t^2+12t^3+3t^4\). No universal one-parameter generating function, spectrum, or recurrence is asserted for the class. The short finite sequences are identification checks, not evidence of priority; no OEIS search of an invented infinite continuation was used.

## 3. Inspected primary sources and collisions excluded

### [XX26] The originating conjecture

Yan-Ting Xie and Shou-Jun Xu, *Ultra log-concavity and real-rootedness of dependence polynomials*, Electronic Journal of Combinatorics **33**(4) (2026), #P4.9, DOI [10.37236/13310](https://doi.org/10.37236/13310). Full author version: [arXiv:2408.09152v2](https://arxiv.org/html/2408.09152v2), dated 10 October 2026.

**Inspected:** Section 4, Proposition 4.2, the augmentation definition, Conjecture 4.4, and Example 4.5. Conjecture 4.4 asks for ULC for the entire additive parameter-two class; no rank-four or seven-element resolution is stated there. Example 4.5 concerns parameter three. Its failure of log-concavity is consequently outside the supplement's hypotheses. This source supplies the target and exact terminology, not evidence that no subsequent solution exists.

### [KN11] General conversion theorems and the small ordinary-matroid result

Jeff Kahn and Michael Neiman, *A strong log-concavity property for measures on Boolean algebras*, Journal of Combinatorial Theory, Series A **118**(6) (2011), 1749–1760, DOI [10.1016/j.jcta.2011.02.007](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.jcta.2011.02.007). Full text: [arXiv:0907.0243](https://arxiv.org/pdf/0907.0243).

**Inspected:** definitions, Theorems 1, 7–9, and the complete Section 5 proof. Theorem 7 assumes an **ordinary matroid** and bounds the **ground-set size** by eleven. Its proof invokes matroid rank, coloops, and Dowling's matroid lemma. It does not state a theorem for relaxed gap-two augmentation. Theorem 1 supplies CAPP-to-ULC, and Theorem 9 supplies the initial-five conclusion from APP under all conditionings leaving two, four, or six coordinates. The same theorem also supplies the terminal five normalized coefficients. These conversion steps are established results and should receive explicit attribution.

The first fingerprint separates the classes: \(\{3\}\) cannot be augmented toward \(\{1,2\}\), so ordinary augmentation fails, although gap-two augmentation holds. Thus ground-set size three alone cannot license applying Theorem 7 to this class.

### [ALGV24] Full Mason ULC for ordinary matroids

Nima Anari, Kuikui Liu, Shayan Oveis Gharan, and Cynthia Vinzant, *Log-concave polynomials III: Mason's ultra-log-concavity conjecture for independent sets of matroids*, Proceedings of the American Mathematical Society **152**(5) (2024), 1969–1981, DOI [10.1090/proc/16724](https://doi.org/10.1090/proc/16724). Full author version: [arXiv:1811.01600](https://arxiv.org/pdf/1811.01600).

**Inspected:** Theorem 1.2, Section 2.1 definitions, and the Section 4 passage using parallel classes. The matroid axiom is augmentation for every strict size inequality. Full ULC in that subclass is established and is not a new part of the supplement. Merely applying that theorem to a downset satisfying only the weaker axiom would be invalid. The complete proof was not re-audited for this gate.

### Recent matroid-intersection pointer

Adam Schweitzer, *A note on the ultra log-concavity of matroid intersection*, [arXiv:2608.23262](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.23262), submitted 24 August 2026. The indexed primary abstract announces ULC for \(M_2^\natural\)-concave functions and, in particular, common independent sets of two matroids. Direct abstract/PDF/HTML retrieval returned access errors; its full theorem and proof were **not inspected**.

The intersection-of-two-matroids specialization does not subsume all systems in this supplement. For the fourth fingerprint, every singleton is feasible. If it were the common-independent-set family of two ordinary matroids, neither could have a loop. In each matroid, dependent pairs form disjoint cliques of parallel elements (the equivalence relation is recorded in [ALGV24, §2.1]). Their union would have to be \(C_5\). Since \(C_5\) is triangle-free, each such clique union is a matching; two matchings cannot cover an odd cycle. This excludes an object-identity shortcut through that specialization. It does not replace inspection of the stronger announced theorem.

Another located primary-author pointer was Alex Chengyu Li, *Additive Augmentation Gaps Do Not Force Log-Concavity*, [SSRN 7434141](https://papers.ssrn.com/sol3/papers.cfm?abstract_id=7434141), September 2026. Its accessible abstract/author summary concerns augmentation parameter three, including a rank-four counterexample. The full proof was not inspected here. Confusing rank four with augmentation parameter four or three would produce a false collision.

| Historical priority | **Bounded uncertainty** | Failed searches do not establish either. |

The current analytic proof uses the main manuscript's graph antipodal-pairs theorem in an essential case. It is therefore an application of that theorem to a further conjectured class, followed by established conversion machinery. The exhaustive certificate remains corroborating evidence; it is not the source of a universal proof claim.

Permitted wording is: “We prove weighted ULC and CAPP for additive 2-matroids of rank at most four and for those on at most seven elements; no earlier result for these exact classes was found in the bounded search recorded here.” Avoid “first,” “previously unknown,” or an unconditional claim that all possible prior literature was excluded. Before a publication-level priority claim, the unresolved full-text pointer and the possibility of equivalent older augmentation terminology merit specialist verification. No author was contacted.
